# Compliance and Quality

## Certification Strategy

Identify requirements from:

Product + Technology + Battery + Market

Potential requirements include:

### United States
- FCC
- UL / ETL where applicable
- DOE where applicable

### European Union
- CE
- RED
- EMC
- LVD
- RoHS
- REACH
- WEEE

### Japan
- TELEC / GITEKI
- PSE where applicable

### Battery
- UN38.3
- IEC 62133 where applicable

### Wireless Charging
- Qi / Qi2 where relevant

Certification requirements must be confirmed with an accredited laboratory or compliance specialist.

## DFM

Mechanical checks:

- Wall thickness
- Draft angle
- Undercuts
- Snap fits
- Screw count
- Assembly direction
- Cosmetic surfaces

Electronics checks:

- PCB panelization
- Test points
- Component spacing
- SMT risk
- Connector placement

## DFT

Define production tests as relevant:

- Power-on
- Current
- Voltage
- RF
- Bluetooth
- Wi-Fi
- Charging
- Battery
- Buttons
- LEDs
- Sensors
- Audio
- Firmware version

## Reliability

Consider:

- Drop
- Vibration
- Temperature
- Thermal cycling
- Humidity
- USB insertion
- Button life
- Switch life
- Charging cycles
- Battery aging
- Cable bending
- Surface abrasion

## FMEA

Evaluate:

- Failure mode
- Effect
- Severity
- Occurrence
- Detection
- Risk

For critical issues use:

Root Cause → Containment → Corrective Action → Preventive Action
