# Opportunity Scoring

## Objective

Rank opportunity spaces and product concepts without pretending uncertain qualitative evidence is precise quantitative data.

## Two-Level Scoring

Score the problem opportunity before scoring the product concept.

### Level 1 — Problem Opportunity

Evaluate:

- Frequency
- Severity / consequence
- Importance of the job
- Dissatisfaction with existing solutions
- Workaround / behavioral-cost signal
- Willingness-to-pay signal
- Evidence strength

### Level 2 — Product Opportunity

Evaluate:

- User value
- Differentiation
- Technical feasibility
- Cost feasibility
- Margin potential
- Manufacturing feasibility
- Compliance feasibility
- Channel fit
- Competitive defensibility

## Rating Method

When evidence is qualitative, use:

- High
- Medium
- Low
- Unknown

When a sufficiently structured dataset exists, numeric scoring may be used, but show assumptions and methodology.

Never invent precision.

## Opportunity Priority

A strong candidate generally combines:

High User Importance × High Dissatisfaction × Strong Evidence × Good Solvability × Attractive Economics

## Confidence

Every opportunity should have a confidence level:

- High confidence
- Medium confidence
- Low confidence

Confidence depends on source quality, sample breadth, consistency across sources, and validation depth.

## Kill Criteria

Downgrade or reject an opportunity if one or more critical conditions apply:

- Problem evidence is weak
- Existing products already solve the job well
- Users are unwilling to change behavior or pay
- Target economics cannot work
- Required technology is immature or too costly
- Compliance burden destroys the business case
- Manufacturing risk is disproportionate
- Differentiation is easily copied and price competition is severe

| Rank | Opportunity Space | User / Scene | Problem Evidence | User Value | Differentiation | Feasibility | Economics | Confidence | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Handoff

Take the top 1–3 opportunities into `/validate`.

Do not generate a full PRD from an opportunity ranking alone.
