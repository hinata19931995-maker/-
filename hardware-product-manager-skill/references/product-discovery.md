# Product Discovery

## Objective

Determine whether a meaningful product opportunity exists before writing a full PRD.

## Opportunity Discovery

Assess:

- Target market
- Target country
- Target user
- Usage scenario
- Existing alternatives
- Pain points
- Market maturity
- Price bands
- Competitive intensity
- Opportunity gaps

## Scenario Definition

Use:

Person + Place + Time + Task + Problem

Separate:

- Core scenario
- Secondary scenario
- Edge scenario

## JTBD

Format:

When ______, I want to ______, so that ______.

Classify:

- Functional jobs
- Emotional jobs
- Social jobs

## Pain Point Prioritization

Evaluate:

- Frequency
- Severity
- Existing solution
- Solution quality
- Willingness to pay
- Opportunity

Priority:

- P0 Critical
- P1 Important
- P2 Nice to solve

Do not invent pain points to justify the product.

## Competitive Analysis

Analyze:

1. Direct competitors
2. Indirect competitors
3. Substitute behaviors
4. Emerging alternatives

Never fabricate price, sales, reviews, market share, or specs.

## Review Mining

Classify complaints into:

- Performance
- Reliability
- Compatibility
- UX
- Noise
- Heat
- Battery
- Charging
- Materials
- Durability
- Size
- Installation
- Instructions
- Packaging
- Customer expectations

Prioritize using:

Complaint Frequency × Complaint Severity × Solvability

## Opportunity Mapping

Classify opportunities as:

- Performance
- UX
- Reliability
- Cost
- Design
- Scenario
- Ecosystem

Prioritize using:

User Value × Differentiation × Feasibility × Margin Potential
